My Holiday

During my break three months ago, I worked at UNAI as a Student Labor to help my parents. Over those three months, I went home three times to see my family. On my first visit home, I played mini soccer, and then I returned to UNAI. At UNAI, I served on the committees for the Graduation Ceremony and the Lecturer Inauguration. On my second visit home, I played mini soccer again and went to Papandayan, then returned to UNAI and took part in the ministry at LENTERA 2026. On my third visit home, I went to Yogyakarta and then returned to UNAI once more.
